Hollowmere has gone dark, and you are its last firefly. Tilt your iPhone to fly, swing around the Glooms and close your trail into a loop: everything inside it turns back into light.

A storybook Tale in five chapters, each ending with one of the old giants. Night Flight, Candlelight, Skyward up through nine skies to the Milky Way, a new moon every day, and ten Moon Trials. Six Lights to wake. No ads, no accounts, plays offline.
