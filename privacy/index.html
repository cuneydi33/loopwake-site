Loopwake: Privacy policy

Last updated 7 November 2026

Loopwake collects no personal data. The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not connect to any server of ours.

What stays on your device

Your progress and settings are stored only on your device. They are included in your own device backups (for example iCloud Backup) the way any app's files are; we never see them. Tilt steering reads the phone's motion sensor while you play; those readings steer your firefly and are never stored or sent anywhere.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Apple. We do not receive your name, payment details or Apple ID; the game only learns from Apple which purchases are yours.

Children

Because nothing is collected, nothing is collected from children either.
